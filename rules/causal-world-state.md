# Causal World State

## Minimum sufficient authority

Any world object capable of changing a consequential outcome must have sufficient authoritative state for the causal role being resolved, or runtime must route to a registered deeper owner/materialization process before resolving that outcome. Missing detail is never silently neutral, favorable, hostile, or retroactively chosen to fit a revealed plan.

This applies to people, institutional offices, strategic sites, forces, logistics, detention, medical care, barriers, communications, transport, reserves, administration, intelligence infrastructure and other persistent world functions.

## Three resolution levels

1. **Ontology:** the thing exists. Static ambient content may establish ordinary locations, institutions, facilities or office concepts when no mutable mechanical state is presently needed.
2. **Compact operational state:** persist only the minimum state needed for fair offscreen and near-screen simulation.
3. **Materialized authority:** load or create the registered deeper owner when individual identity, detailed facility state, exact force composition, stock, equipment, techniques, relationships, knowledge or another finer-grained fact becomes consequential.

Compression changes storage and retrieval cost only. It may never improve capability, survival, readiness, recovery, resources, access, security, development, succession or outcomes.

## Institutional role slots

A role slot represents the continuity of an institutional office while its incumbent does not yet require a persistent person owner. It is not a hidden character.

An occupied unmaterialized role slot may persist only institutionally necessary continuity facts: office identity and authority scope, a stable incumbency ID, incumbent age band or years in role, functional capability band, health and operational availability, service-development credit, retirement/vacancy/succession status and last settlement timestamp.

It may not persist a secret name, personality, biography, private relationships, private inventory, exact techniques, exact chakra, exact combat statistics or other person-level facts merely to prebuild an NPC.

Role-slot time settlement may age the incumbent band, accumulate lawful service-development credit, change availability, cause retirement/vacancy or trigger succession according to registered institutional processes. Such settlement must not secretly resolve person-specific events that require a real person owner.

When the incumbent becomes individually consequential, materialize the same incumbent using the slot's stable incumbency ID. Materialization must conserve institutional continuity, population/manpower where applicable, accumulated service evidence and already-resolved outcomes. It may fill previously unknown individual detail only under the normal person-materialization rules and may not retroactively optimize or sabotage an earlier result.

A materialized incumbent keeps the office slot as institutional authority. The slot references the person; the person does not replace the office's institutional state.

## Strategic places

Static ambient locations own ontology and scene/travel reference only. A place becomes mutable strategic state when persistent topology, security, capacity, damage, access, custody, utilities, medical function, detention, communications, stores, logistics, repair, emergency readiness or another site property can materially affect outcomes.

A strategic place records only the causal modules it actually needs. It does not become a universal fortress sheet. Detailed personnel, units, stock, inventories and mechanics remain in their own owners and are referenced rather than duplicated.

Examples of causal modules include security/watch coverage, barrier coverage, treatment capacity, detention capacity, communications, utilities, supply storage, repair capability, transport handling, administrative throughput and emergency reserves. A module is required only when that function is consequential.

If compact place state cannot answer a consequential question, runtime must deepen through its registered authority references or materialize the missing lawful detail before resolution. A facility label such as `hospital`, `armory`, `gate` or `prison` never grants unstated capacity, guards, supplies, topology or readiness.

## Temporal fairness

Role slots and strategic-site operational modules participate in normal temporal settlement when their state can change with elapsed time. Their `last_settled_at` values are domain evidence, not a second general scheduler. Exact due boundaries and causal wake-ups remain owned by the temporal runtime and relevant process contracts.

Offscreen compression must produce the same result as equivalent exact settlement given the same authoritative starting state, elapsed time and registered random inputs.
