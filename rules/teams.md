# Teams and Assignment

Team existence, roster, instructor, leadership, status, doctrine and assignments are mutable campaign state and must be read from `state/team/` plus the current assignment/process owners.

A graduation, promotion, recommendation or proposed roster does not itself create an active team. Activation requires lawful assignment authority, a committed roster, effective time, and any required acceptance/orders. Team roles and leadership may change through later lawful state changes.

Do not infer a team from old rules, chat plans, character compatibility, or mission convenience.

## Team types and special cells

Reusable legal shapes live in `data/team/team-types.json`. A special mission team may support a flexible named roster when lawful authority and member obligations permit it. This capability never creates a roster by itself. Team doctrine, tendencies, training, loadout rules, communications, contingencies, resource rules, and standing orders become canonical only after an actual team exists and the lawful authority adopts them.

A pending team remains pending. ANBU candidacy is not active ANBU authority.
