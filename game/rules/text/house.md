# House Tang and Sword Manor

Current House membership, leadership, delegated authority, charter status, internal policy, formations, stocks and facilities are mutable campaign state under `state/house/` and related owners. Do not copy current names, counts, offices or assignments into this rule file.

House ranks, formation templates, and stable loadout definitions are routed through `game/data/house/`. Promotion changes authority/access according to the current House state and does not grant statistics for free.

Every House member is a real persistent person. Exact owners and rostered person cores expose the same full logical-sheet interface; a rostered core shares routine capability/development state with its referenced House cohort until real individual divergence requires a component. External or village forces assigned under House or player command retain their original personnel ownership unless a lawful transfer changes it.

Permanent organization changes require current House authority or saved delegation. The narrator may not create formations, commanders, doctrine, memberships or permanent loadouts merely for convenience.
