# Exact Combat — Live GM Entry

**Immediately read `references/combat-base.md` in full before directing or narrating exact combat.** That file is the complete combat contract. The rules in this entry are later incident-derived overrides and therefore take precedence wherever wording in the base reference is ambiguous or conflicts with them.

## Canonical combat narration authority

When the current-transition projection provides `combat_narrative`, its ordered `material_beats` are the **primary scene spine**. Use its routine/event/resource summaries only to compress repetition around those material beats. In that case, `narrative_projection` is a secondary compatibility/fallback surface and must never replace, reorder, or erase material beats from `combat_narrative`.

When a committed exact-combat result exposes `narrative_projection` **and no richer current-transition `combat_narrative` is available**, treat the timestamped beats as the primary player-facing chronology for that resolved span. A beat marked `must_narrate_before_next_decision: true` remains mandatory before control returns. A coarse `contact_zone: neck` is not permission to name unsupported structures, and withdrawal must be physically staged before summarizing the enemy as retreating or routing.

Never select whichever projection produces the shortest or easiest prose. If two deterministic projections differ in detail, consume the richer material chronology and use the narrower one only for compatibility checks or compression.

## Repetition, stagnation, and control cadence

A runtime exchange, preview, execute, receipt, or refresh boundary is **not** a narrative scene boundary and is **not** a player-decision boundary. If Wei's declared or standing combat intent still answers the situation, continue that intent through the next lawful bounded resolution instead of manufacturing a menu because one exchange ended or one tactic failed once.

Repeated mechanically similar exchanges must be compressed. If Wei makes the same lawful attempt several times with the same result, do not write three versions of "he attacks, he is interrupted". State the repeated pressure once, then spend prose on the material changes that actually occurred: a new wound, withdrawal, opening, positional change, resource shift, weapon change, collapse, rescue pressure, surrender, or other committed development. If nothing material changes, compress harder rather than padding.

Do not create a "sharper choice" merely because a tactic is currently ineffective. Return control only when the standing intent no longer resolves a genuine new tradeoff, a protected beat requires it, the player-delegated span ends, Wei cannot continue, or another real strategic/voluntary commitment appears. Mechanical frustration is not itself a protected decision.

The scene must answer **why the fight changed shape**, not merely enumerate that attacks were blocked or rejected. Player-facing combat prose translates mechanics into lived action: **Player-facing combat prose must not name resolver primitives** such as **attack line**, **movement lane**, or **contact geometry** when ordinary physical language works. **Translate mechanics into embodied cause and effect** and preserve the **continuity of an action scene in a strong novel or film**. The receipt is evidence for the GM, not dialogue for the player.

## Preserved base-contract anchors

These incident overrides do not weaken any existing combat rule. In particular:

- **Preserve independent components of a compound combat declaration** such as **rally them and attack**; do not silently reduce a compound player intent to one mechanical component.
- `confirmed_observed_hostile_count` is cumulative encounter observation. **It is not a live census**. **Current-strength narration must come from fresh player-lawful battlefield evidence**; **Never back-calculate an IC live enemy count** from hidden totals, and **fresh lawful perception outranks stale mission reports**.
- **Exact anatomy language is evidence-bound**. A coarse hit location is never **permission to say the blade pierced the throat** or another unsupported structure.
- **Successful escape is not itself a casualty**; casualty, withdrawal, escape, incapacitation, and death remain distinct committed outcomes.
- Protected casualty, surrender, ceasefire, mission-conflict, and other genuine agency checkpoints retain their base-reference rules unchanged.
