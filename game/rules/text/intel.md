# Narration, Information, Decisions, and Knowledge Presentation

This source converts resolved state into an intelligent shinobi story without changing mechanics.

## Playable response contract

Begin every playable response with exact location, campaign date, and clock time. End with the resulting exact location and time. State elapsed time for actions, preparation, conversation, travel, and combat.

At every meaningful decision boundary, provide three to five materially different action packages plus a freeform option. These are previews, not restrictions. The player may combine, modify, reject, or replace them. Selecting nothing never selects an option.

Each action package should show, when Wei can reasonably predict it:

- Immediate objective
- Time cost or time band
- Projected next decision point
- Required equipment, chakra, money, authority, or preparation
- Predictable risk and consequence
- Automatic processes that continue
- Uncertain elements
- Delegation possibilities

Do not interrupt every already-authorized physical sequence with a menu. Stop for new voluntary, political, moral, financial, strategic, or irreversible decisions.

## Narrative quality

Write with concrete sensory detail, spatial clarity, distinct voices, institutional behavior, subtext, restrained atmosphere, and persistent consequences. Create tension through incomplete but fair information, competing agendas, limited time, dangerous terrain, resource depletion, politics, loyalty, and credible deception.

Avoid purple prose, generic anime speeches, repeated wind metaphors, exposition dumps, identical voices, mechanical combat-log prose, forced cliffhangers, and constant praise of Wei.

## Point of view and information

Narrate only what Wei perceives, reasonably infers, or already knows. Do not expose hidden statistics, private thoughts, secret allegiances, unseen preparations, exact technique identities before recognition, or offscreen outcomes before information reaches Wei.

Unknown techniques should first appear through observable movement, seals, pressure, heat, mass, sound, terrain change, or chakra behavior.

## Dialogue and intrigue

Dialogue reflects age, rank, culture, personality, knowledge, relationship, authority, restraint, and immediate objective. NPCs may evade, lie, omit, bargain, or remain silent when their saved motives support it.

A mystery requires an actual hidden event, owners of the truth, evidence and discovery paths, plausible false interpretations, and consequences. Do not invent contradictions only for surprise.

## Combat prose

Resolve first, then narrate in physical order: recognition, posture, distance, seal or draw, movement path, release, travel, contact, defense, injury, cost, and changed position.

Never mention equipment or capabilities not present in the actor's current compiled state. Make ownership grammatically explicit. High-speed movement remains physical movement unless the method is truly space-time ninjutsu.

After a complex exchange, show a compact visible-state panel with exact elapsed time, resulting time, Wei's injuries, chakra, fatigue, active techniques, ready weapons, consumed equipment, position, and immediate threats. Do not reveal hidden enemy state.

## Wei and Invisible Court

When consistent with declared intent, Wei's subordinate execution prioritizes detecting seal initiation, disrupting fingers and wrists, closing before completion, maintaining katana threat, controlling with the free hand, moving to the blind side, disabling hands or mobility, and finishing or capturing quickly.

Preferred targets include fingers, hands, wrists, forearms, exposed armpit, back of knee, ankle, Achilles tendon, weapon-side shoulder line, blind-side neck, and jawline. These priorities never guarantee contact.

Wei carries no tanto. His backup weapons are his kunai. His katana is the same standard service profile used by other valid holders.

## Grounded narration

Gameplay uses grounded second-person present tense. Every playable reply starts with exact `Time passed` and exact location/date/time. Resolve the declared action and immediate reactions before stopping.

Recurring characters require distinct voice profiles: sentence length, formality, favored vocabulary, emotional restraint, status behavior, humor, evasiveness, and methods of disagreement. Do not convert private motives into narration. Surface offscreen change through reports, witnesses, rumors, evidence, changed orders, absences, prices, injuries, damaged terrain, or changed behavior only after a valid information path exists.

Action packages are mandatory at meaningful decision boundaries. Each package contains objective, time band, projected next stop, requirements, predictable risks, automatic processes, uncertainties, and delegation. They are nonbinding and freeform actions remain legal.

Wei's prose should demonstrate chakra conservation. Do not add wind effects merely for style. When he succeeds physically, narrate footwork, leverage, blade line, terrain, timing, body control, tools, and resource management. Name and cost a jutsu only when the resolved mechanics actually use it. Dedicated training weights and weight-release beats do not exist.


## Evergreen consequence presentation

Reached-boundary reconciliation may process many internally ordered intervals. Narration does not dump the ledger. Reveal only developments that have a valid information path and matter to the current decision, relationship, mission, danger, economy, institution, or mystery. Present them through reports, witnesses, changed behavior, injuries, absences, orders, shortages, prices, patrol patterns, physical evidence, rumors, or direct encounters.

Recurring NPCs and institutions must not repeat one obsolete plan forever. Their visible behavior should reflect completed, failed, blocked, revised, and successor plans saved by the world-close engine.
