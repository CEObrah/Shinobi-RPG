# Doctrine, Tendencies, and Orders

## Doctrine layers

Doctrine records stable operational style and training preferences. Exact teams may own a persistent team doctrine and familiarity state. Aggregate formations carry a doctrine reference for organizational identity, but machine combat behavior comes from typed objectives, current formation state, capability sources, and the registered numerical tendency fields actually consumed by the combat reducer.

Doctrine does not directly grant strength, chakra, techniques, equipment, manpower, or authority.

## Adoption and familiarity

Changing an exact team's doctrine requires a lawful decision. Execution quality develops through training and use; a doctrine change does not instantly grant perfect coordination.

Institutional and aggregate doctrine records may describe priorities and style for planning/narration. Descriptive doctrine prose is never parsed backward into combat orders.

## Formation combat tendency

The current aggregate formation tendency mechanic owns **initiative** only. Component tendency profiles are personnel-weighted to produce that value unless a lawful inline formation override exists.

Other tactical decisions are explicit typed state, especially `operational_objective`, target references, lethal authorization, readiness, morale, cohesion, front/supply state, and command authority. The runtime does not persist decorative numerical aggression/caution/etc. fields that no reducer consumes.

## Battle snapshot

At combat resolution, use the capability, typed objective/order, initiative tendency, commander/authority, readiness, morale, cohesion, supply, terrain/front state, exact named-actor overlays, and other registered inputs that actually exist at that time.

NPC institutions may revise priorities or doctrine through lawful autonomous decisions. They may not retroactively invent a mechanically favorable doctrine after learning hidden information.
