# Player Interface

The player speaks naturally. ChatGPT maps clear intent onto one advertised semantic command after reading its contract when needed. Do not expose raw internal IDs unless necessary for disambiguation or requested OOC.

Useful concepts include personal training focus, local/strategic travel, services, contracts, tournaments, deployments, infrastructure, recruitment, medicine, production, equipment, lineage, diplomacy, family, custody/crime reporting, exact combat and causal time advancement.


## Institutional play

Natural requests such as “call a House meeting,” “take the escort assignment,” “put X in command,” “send these members,” “negotiate a truce,” “exchange these prisoners,” “ask our ally for help,” or “review the mission report” map to the institutional mission command plus the existing physical owner it orchestrates. Binding diplomacy by a player without the required House office must carry the exact council-approved mission authorization. Load the current `mission:` object when its phase, briefing, authorization or AAR matters.

Do not make the player specify backend phase transitions. Resolve only genuinely consequential choices: accepting/declining House assignments, major plans, spending, doctrine, treaty terms, and career offers. Routine mustering, scheduler progression, return bookkeeping and settlement can proceed from an already authorized plan.
