# House Tang and Sword Manor

Current House membership, leadership, delegated authority, charter status, internal policy, formations, stocks and facilities are mutable campaign state under `state/house/` and related owners. Do not copy current names, counts, offices or assignments into this rule file.

House ranks, formation templates, and stable loadout definitions are routed through `game/data/house/`. Promotion changes authority/access according to the current House state and does not grant statistics, techniques, or mastery for free.

Every House member is a real persistent person. Exact owners and rostered person cores expose the same logical-person interface. A rostered House core owns a persistent individual-lite profile containing that person's established capability and development state; House cohorts are organization/training assignments whose summaries are derived from their members. Reassignment, cohort growth, or summary recomputation never rerolls an established person. Exactification adds deeper causal components only when needed and preserves the same identity, profile, provenance, affiliations, and history.

External or village forces assigned under House or player command retain their original personnel ownership unless a lawful transfer changes it. Permanent organization changes require current House authority or saved delegation. The narrator may not create formations, commanders, doctrine, memberships or permanent loadouts merely for convenience.
