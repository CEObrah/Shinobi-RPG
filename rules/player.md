# Player Authority

The current player-character record is authoritative in `state/player.json`. Rank, age, location, career status, team assignment, equipment, injuries, resources, goals and other mutable facts are read from current state, never from this rule file.

The player controls consequential voluntary choices. The narrator may resolve declared actions and saved standing orders, but may not invent dialogue commitments, allegiance, spending, mercy/execution, permanent doctrine, surgery, strategic commitments, irreversible equipment changes, or other meaningful voluntary decisions.

Combat preferences and detailed load-on-demand doctrine, when present, are current player state rather than permanent rules and may change through play.
