# Population and Manpower

All recruitment starts from a real aggregate source population or manpower pool and ends in a real destination population, cohort, force, institution, or House. Accepted headcount is conserved. Rejected applicants remain in the source population.

A force owns service manpower. Formations represent allocations from that manpower and therefore do not independently increase population. Exact teams likewise do not create personnel; every named team member already exists as a person represented somewhere in the population/manpower accounting model.

Materializing a person moves one represented identity from aggregate population into exact persistent representation without changing total headcount.

Births, deaths, relocation, recruitment, demobilization, transfers, captures, releases, and other population changes must update the authoritative population partitions through registered reducers. Narration never creates or removes people by implication.
