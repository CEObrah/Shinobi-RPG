# Character Representation

A persistent person represents one exact identity. Material personal facts such as health, location, relationships, unique techniques, unique equipment, offices, and commitments belong to that person's authoritative state.

Ordinary unnamed personnel do not require individual files. They may remain represented by a force, formation component, or cohort until an identity-dependent consequence makes an exact person necessary.

Materialization creates a stable identity from an existing represented population; it does not add population. Once materialized, the person's continuity remains exact even when they later operate inside a larger formation.

An exact team is a roster of exact people. It is not a person-representation tier and it is not an aggregate manpower pool.
