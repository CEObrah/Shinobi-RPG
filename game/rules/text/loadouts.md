# Loadouts and Equipment Standards

Equipment state distinguishes an **intended standard** from **actual issued inventory**. Declaring a standard never creates equipment.

## Aggregate formations

Formation components do not persist a second per-component loadout ledger. Their troop type and role select the appropriate registered force capability source, which may reference a standard loadout/training/doctrine profile as cold capability provenance.

Actual scarce equipment, ammunition, named assets, shortages, consumption, issue, return, and refit live in the inventory/asset authorities. If a formation or exact person needs a materially different actual equipment state, change the lawful inventory/loadout owner rather than writing another descriptive copy into the formation component.

Named people may have explicit personal equipment exceptions. A refit changes real issued equipment only when the required stock, authority, and time are available; otherwise the equipment is not granted.
