# Scene Playbook

Use these patterns only when the corresponding scene is already supported by fresh runtime context. They guide presentation, not campaign truth. Every pattern inherits `scene-craft.md`'s interaction-first invariant: substantive human contact plays as interaction by default, not as a state digest.

## House councils and mission briefings

Stage the real room and real attendees. Put issuer, authority, commander candidates, known intelligence, objective, time pressure, resources, and disagreement on screen through human interaction instead of a narrator reading a mission object aloud.

A council is not a voting minigame. Advisors may disagree, question, warn, or propose alternatives; the lawful authority makes institutional decisions. Wei still owns his protected acceptance, refusal, personal commitments, and tactical choices unless authority or prior delegation says otherwise.

Do not stage a council as sequential depositions from every named attendee. Let the chair or senior authority control the floor when appropriate. Keep a speaker active across follow-up questions instead of handing every sentence to a new expert. Allow side comments, direct replies, correction, silence, visible reaction, or a subordinate waiting until addressed. An attendee list is not a dialogue queue.

If a direct consequential question is put to Wei, apply `choices.md` before ending the turn.

## Family and relationship scenes

Let kinship, rank, age, established relationship, history, household pressure, affection, resentment, duty, and face shape dialogue. Do not invent Wei's feelings or romantic/family commitments.

## Training scenes

Show technique through body mechanics, correction, repetition, timing, fatigue, Qi control, weapon handling, teaching, and observed limits when supported. Do not fabricate stat gains. A session can be meaningful without a whole-number increase when the runtime records residual progress, familiarity, recovery, doctrine work, or other real development.

## Personal combat and team skirmishes

Keep geometry, timing, facing, fatigue, injury, weapons, Qi, poison, allies, terrain, and line of sight legible. A coarse player command may let standing doctrine fill unspecified details, but explicit player instructions override doctrine.

Do not turn several attackers into independent one-on-one exchanges. Keep shared body state, reaction load, crowding, movement, and committed momentum coherent.

## Travel and escort scenes

Travel is physical. Use current route, distance, time, weather, road/terrain condition, mounts, load, companions, fatigue, known threats, and stops only when established. An escort mission should feel like movement of real people and goods, not a teleport between mission flags.

When a substantive multi-person journey forms or departs and fresh context establishes the co-located participants, normally play the human handoff before compressing the road. Demand-load only the key people who materially matter at that beat, such as the commander, deputy/watch lead, scout, medic, guard, specialist, or permanent retinue member. Let them report readiness, coordinate roles, ask practical questions, react to Wei's orders, and speak to one another. Do not dump an entire roster as introductions, and do not invent personalities merely to make every member speak.

Treat exact movement participants exposed by the Runtime as the traveling cast for that physical movement. Sharing only a route name is never enough to establish co-presence. If movement participants are not exposed, do not manufacture companions from a mission roster or prior chat memory.

Quiet travel is allowed to be genuinely quiet, but quiet is not the same as socially empty. On a long journey, compress routine hours and days aggressively while occasionally expanding a grounded human beat when established co-travelers and current state provide material for one. Useful beats include watch handoffs, scout coordination, medical or equipment checks, discussion of the coming job, training or professional routine, command clarification, ordinary conversation, NPC-to-NPC cross-talk, relationship-informed friction or rapport, and reactions to real changes in progress or conditions. These are sparse samples of lived travel, not mandatory daily events.

Never manufacture bandits, accidents, rumors, weather, arguments, secrets, romance, or other drama merely because several quiet days passed. A journey may contain no hostile encounter at all. Human texture should arise from established people, roles, relationships, observations, routines, and mechanically real developments.

Routine travel under an already-chosen plan can continue without repeated menus. Internal `quiet_frontier_chunk` boundaries are never individual story beats: consume them silently under the standing travel policy rather than narrating each scheduler increment. Expand again when there is a real fork, material contact, route/resource problem, consequential report, meaningful change in physical travel state, or a useful character scene supported by exact co-presence. Departure, a meaningful journey phase change, approach, and arrival are especially strong opportunities for interaction even when no crisis occurs.

Companion dialogue remains reversible scene realization. NPCs may advise, coordinate, joke, object, ask questions, or talk among themselves from player-safe facts, but dialogue cannot invent a binding order, new secret fact, resource transfer, relationship change, or other durable consequence. Wei retains every protected consequential choice.

## Investigations

Separate observation, testimony, rumor, inference, and verified evidence. Let witnesses be incomplete or mistaken for grounded reasons, not because the narrator needs mystery. Do not reveal hidden answers in suggestive narration.

## Negotiation and institutional contact

Keep access, audience, proposal, response, and settlement as distinct causal stages. Let the people with actual authority speak. Do not turn an attempt into acceptance or a meeting into agreement.

## Mission return and AAR

On return, stage the human handoff first when appropriate: commander/reporting member, House authority, wounded/missing people, captives, recovered goods, payment/reward, intelligence, and immediate consequence. Then surface compact structured accounting if useful.

Do not narrate a strike-by-strike replay of an offscreen mission unless Wei lawfully receives that detail and it matters.
