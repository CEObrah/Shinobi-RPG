# Invisible Court

Invisible Court is a learned House Tang martial system and is separate from Guardian Current.

Its foundation emphasizes one-handed katana plus free hand, hand-seal interruption, movement, counterattack, grappling, restraint, disarm recovery, fighting without chakra, protection of another person, formation discipline, first aid, genjutsu recognition, and environmental survival.

Access tiers follow House rank, specialization, and explicit restricted permissions. Direct Tang family may access family-restricted doctrine but do not gain mastery automatically.

Reusable doctrine modules include Court Escort, Court Interdiction, Court Capture, Court Assault, Court Defense, Court Recon, Court Extraction, Manor Defense, and Command Doctrine. These are doctrine building blocks, not automatically created permanent units.
