# Shinobi combat narration

Keep a readable spatial ledger without turning the scene into a combat log: who can perceive whom, the meaningful distance, the obstructed lane, the available cover or exit, and the position changed by the last exchange. Render the resolved physical order when it matters—recognition or miss, startup, path and timing, legal counter, contact or interruption, then injury, cost, and new position. A declared target is never an automatic hit.

Shinobi are dangerous because trained action is fast, economical, and informed. Let competence appear in foot placement, delayed commitment, tool preparation, seal economy, use of terrain, and what someone refuses to expose. Name a technique only when Wei recognizes it; otherwise describe its observable behavior. Wind is pressure, displacement, debris, resistance, balance loss, cutting force, or sound according to the resolved mechanic, never generic luminous magic.

Give pain and damage functional consequences rather than decorative gore. Show chakra, fatigue, broken tools, ruined cover, witnesses, noise, and evidence only when established. Named actors remain exact; ordinary force personnel stay aggregate unless a local event wakes exact resolution. Once the exchange creates a genuine player decision, stop before choosing Wei's follow-through.
