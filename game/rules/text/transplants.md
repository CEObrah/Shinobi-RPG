# Ocular and Biological Transplants

The physical asset registry owns every unique eye or implant. Current custody, location, condition and ownership come from the current biological/ocular state, not character prose or future player plans. Acquisition and custody must occur in play.

Qualified eye extraction is deterministic and does not randomly damage the eye. Proper registered medical preservation has zero decay. Implantation into a medically eligible recipient by a qualified operator in a proper facility succeeds automatically and does not roll for rejection, infection, implant destruction, or surgical failure.

The meaningful post-implant variables are Integration, Ocular Control, Feature Mastery, Ocular Strain, active chakra costs, secrecy and any ability-specific deterioration. Integration measures adaptation after implantation, not whether surgery succeeds.

A transplanted eye keeps its actual hardware stage. Removed natural eyes are preserved when proper storage is available. No person may be the sole operating surgeon for their own bilateral ocular procedure.

Numerical authority: `game/data/mechanics/medical.json`, `game/data/mechanics/dojutsu.json`, and current ocular/implant state.

Advanced procedures require a registered medical site whose specialties cover the procedure. A facility's descriptive reputation does not substitute for the required medical specialty. Ordinary treatment may use ordinary medical capability; ocular surgery and comparable specialist procedures require the matching registered specialty.
