# Clans, Houses, and Parallel Training

## Institutional role

A shinobi clan or house may be a family, military lineage, economic household, political institution, specialist school, or several of these at once. Village membership and clan membership are overlapping authorities rather than mutually exclusive identities. A Konoha shinobi may serve a village team while also receiving lawful family instruction, maintaining clan obligations, or using clan facilities. The same applies in every other village and country.

The static clan catalog is reference authority only. Current clan heads, elders, rosters, branches, compounds, stock, instructors, politics, casualties, and access rules belong to mutable campaign owners when they become causal. Never infer those facts merely because a later canon article names them.

## Clan training is parallel, not free

Clan training uses the same generic training reducer as village, team, mentor, institutional, and self-directed training. There is no separate clan XP and no surname bonus.

A clan-training interval must prove the same causal inputs as any serious training:

- elapsed time and actual attendance;
- current health, fatigue, strain, and recovery eligibility;
- a lawful instructor or a curriculum that permits self-practice;
- available facility capacity, tools, animals, medicines, weapons, seals, or other resources when relevant;
- curriculum relevance and difficulty fit;
- membership, household, branch, rank, initiation, or permission when the technique is restricted;
- hereditary or biological eligibility when the method depends on a kekkei genkai or other inherited trait;
- current technique knowledge separately from biological eligibility.

One elapsed hour cannot yield simultaneous full training credit from a clan and a village team. If two institutions co-sponsor the same session, resolve one training interval with the actual instructors, facilities, curriculum, and participants rather than double-crediting it.

## Cohorts and offscreen development

Routine clan development should use bounded cohorts or other representation-neutral hosts when members share the same causal training conditions. Do not materialize every cousin or trainee merely to tick training. Split a cohort when injury, assignment, instructor access, technique eligibility, branch status, promotion, war, resource shortage, or another causal difference changes outcomes.

Cold clans do not globally tick. A clan becomes an active autonomous training host only when mutable campaign state establishes enough current authority to schedule real instruction. Once active, its training process may wake on registered boundaries and must conserve instructor time, facility capacity, equipment, health, and member availability.

## Secret techniques and inherited traits

Keep these separate:

1. clan membership or ancestry;
2. hereditary capacity or awakened expression;
3. permission/access to restricted teaching;
4. knowledge of a technique;
5. practiced mastery.

Do not turn a surname into a completed technique sheet. A Hyūga member does not automatically know every Gentle Fist form; an Uchiha does not automatically awaken Sharingan or master Great Fireball; an Uzumaki does not automatically know fūinjutsu; a Kaguya does not automatically possess Shikotsumyaku.

## Cross-institution authority

Clan authority does not automatically supersede Hokage, Kage, mission, academy, military, police, hospital, or team authority. External assignment may reduce time available for clan training. Conversely, a village assignment does not erase lawful family obligations or private training unless current authority or scheduling says so. Resolve conflicts through persisted authority, commitments, and time.

## Source and timeline fairness

The clan catalog intentionally includes lineages from manga, anime, novels, games, and later-era material so no village receives special data privilege. Every record carries provenance. Supplemental material remains supplemental, and future-era facts remain future reference until the campaign reaches or independently establishes them.

If a clan is too sparsely documented for consequential leadership, population, resources, training access, or politics, deepen the institution through registered campaign authority rather than selecting convenient details from model memory.
