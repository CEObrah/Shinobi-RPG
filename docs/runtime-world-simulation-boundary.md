# Runtime World-Simulation Boundary

## Constitution

The runtime is the laws of the Jianghu world, not the menu of possible fictional actions.

Player and NPC intent is open-world. Hard consequences are closed-world and must be resolved through authoritative mechanics. The absence of a bespoke command can block an unsupported persistent consequence, but it must never make ordinary conversation or reversible scene behavior impossible.

## Intent before mechanics

Interpret the full natural-language declaration before loading a mechanic family. Preserve actor, target, method, spoken words, constraints, goal, sequence, and concurrency actually supplied by the player. Never add success, consent, obedience, fear, injury, access, agreement, or another external outcome to player-authored intent.

Only after the attempt is understood should mechanic discovery identify the consequence resolvers that matter. `get_play_context` therefore exposes compact mechanic families rather than a full operation list. Exact operation names and contracts are demand-loaded only when a hard consequence needs adjudication.

Examples:

- `Why are you attacking us?` is a live conversational move even during an established combat/parley surface. The opposing side can answer, lie, refuse, threaten, or bargain according to lawful cognition; combat mechanics still own all hard effects.
- `I punch him` is a physical attack attempt. Exact combat resolves it whether or not a command named `punch` exists.
- `I put the bowl on his head` is normally scene realization. `I smash the bowl into his skull` implicates combat, contact physics, equipment/improvised object effects where registered, anatomy, injury, fatigue, witnesses, and possibly social/legal consequences.
- A compound declaration can contain speech, local movement, and a physical attack. Do not erase the speech because the attack requires exact combat.

## Conversation

Conversation is free; consequences are governed.

An established, physically present NPC may answer, acknowledge, ask, object, advise, joke, interrupt, speculate from lawful evidence, bargain nonbindingly, refuse to explain, or speak to another present person without a bespoke command. Response-bearing conversational moves are generic threads, not only literal questions. Requests, petitions, offers, proposals, and similar moves can remain live until responded to or abandoned with the scene.

Attributed speech proves what was said, not that the statement is objectively true. Salient reversible scene facts may preserve observed local actions, object placement, positioning, visible reactions, shared premises, and incidental details across fresh chats, but they are authority-false continuity records only. Binding surrender, ceasefire, safe passage, custody, payment, ownership, contract, mission disposition, travel, injury, relationship change, or another hard consequence remains with its authoritative mechanic.

## GM-private omniscience, NPC cognition, and combat motive

Player-visible knowledge and GM direction context are different things.

The runtime may expose explicitly marked **GM-private current-scene truth** that Tang Wei does not know. That packet may include hidden identities, exact positions, real wounds, tactics, private goals, encounter motives, causal decision context, and other information the AI needs to direct a coherent scene. The restriction applies to **player-facing disclosure**, not to the director's ability to understand the world.

For route attacks, the active contact retains bounded private motive/intention and decision provenance generated by the autonomous world systems. Combat context may expose that causality directly to the GM alongside exact combat state. The GM may use it to decide why attackers press, hesitate, bluff, negotiate, protect a wounded ally, or answer a question. It becomes Wei's knowledge only through perception, inference, lawful information delivery, or actual NPC disclosure.

Attributed speech still proves only what was said. A liar's statement does not rewrite objective truth. Hard consequences still come from mechanics. GM-private omniscience is a **direction layer**, not a second state authority and not permission for omniscient player narration.

## Fact levels

1. **Ephemeral presentation**: posture, tone, pauses, ordinary local handling and positioning. No persistent write required.
2. **Scene continuity**: authority-false scene sessions, combat-parley threads, attributed speech, shared premises, and salient reversible scene facts. These preserve interaction and local staging without becoming mechanical truth.
3. **Adjudicated consequences**: contested results from exact combat, social/institutional mechanics, travel, investigation, etc.
4. **Hard campaign truth**: time, injury, death, Qi/fatigue state, money, equipment, custody, travel, training gains, people, deployments, factions, missions, contracts, relationships when mechanically significant, and other durable facts.

Narration may realize levels 1-2 inside fresh lawful context. Only mechanics and transactions establish levels 3-4.

## Deterministic longitudinal simulation remains authoritative

Do not weaken the systems that motivated the runtime. Off-screen training, development, recovery, fatigue/rest settlement, faction/institution activity, route operations, escort work, economy, projects, recruitment/population conservation, missions, tournaments, travel, and scheduled causal work continue through runtime chronology whether or not the GM is currently narrating those people.

Screen time must never determine mechanical development.

## Transaction boundary

Persistent writes retain exact revision checks, preview/execute integrity, idempotent request IDs, conservation, physical-presence validation, authority/knowledge validation, WAL/receipts, and durable commit. A natural-language declaration may span reversible scene realization plus sequential exact mechanical writes under standing intent. Each hard write remains its own authoritative transaction unless/until a separately designed composite transaction owner exists.

Do not simulate unsupported hard consequences in prose. If a consequential step is unsupported, stop at that consequence while preserving the ordinary scene and player attempt that were already valid.

## Anti-regression rules

A proposed feature or fix is architecturally suspect if it:

- adds a bespoke command merely so a person can utter or understand an ordinary sentence;
- treats command names as the action vocabulary available to the player;
- sends the full operation list on every live turn when a compact family index suffices;
- requires a state write for every sentence, gesture, or reversible local action;
- lets attributed speech or scene facts mutate objective hard truth;
- treats GM-private truth as player knowledge, hidden-thought narration, or choice premises;
- lets narration bypass exact combat, physical presence, fatigue, Qi, poison, economy, custody, time, knowledge, conservation, or persistence;
- makes off-screen growth depend on narration.

Release tests should preserve both master invariants:

1. Absence of a bespoke runtime command never makes an otherwise plausible ordinary conversation or reversible action impossible.
2. AI narration alone never creates a hard mechanical consequence without adjudication and commitment by its authoritative owner.
