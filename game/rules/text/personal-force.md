# Personal and Delegated Forces

A **force** owns the manpower pool. A commander may receive lawful authority over some of that manpower without becoming its population owner.

A **formation** is the operational body created from an allocated portion of a force. Its components separate materially different troop types, operational roles, condition/rank mixes, or registered initiative tendencies when the distinction affects aggregate computation or conservation.

Assignment changes command authority, not population ownership. Personnel remain accounted for under their source force unless a real transfer transaction changes ownership.

Named officers and other consequential people remain persistent individuals. Ordinary personnel remain aggregate inside their force/formation representation until identity-dependent consequences require an exact person or team.

A temporary detachment records its source force, headcount, composition, assignment, command relationship, location, and lifecycle state. Equipment remains in the appropriate inventory/asset authority instead of being duplicated as component state. Detaching personnel does not create them.

Returning a detachment restores surviving personnel to a lawful force availability partition. Return does not reset casualties, injuries, experience, equipment, cohesion, promotions, or history. Reconstitution requires real replacement resources.
