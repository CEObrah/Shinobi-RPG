# Covert, intelligence, and mission narration

Build tension from bounded perception and elapsed time, never from an omniscient hint. Prioritize sight lines, sound, cover, patrol rhythm, disguises, documents, seals, witnesses, evidence, custody, escape routes, and travel or extraction time. Establish only the operational details Wei can observe or lawfully receive; an unexplained silence may be a fact without becoming proof of an ambush.

Competence is often subtraction: the footstep not taken, the question not asked twice, a seal left untouched, a route abandoned before it becomes a trap. Distinguish observation, suspicion, working inference, rumor, and proof in the prose without labeling them like UI categories. Unknown people remain descriptions until identified.

Success can still leave a footprint. Entry changes dust, displaced guards remember faces, documents have custody, techniques leave evidence, prisoners require transport and jurisdiction, and extraction consumes time. Do not invent a hidden complication to punish a clean result; carry forward only consequences produced by authority and resolution.
