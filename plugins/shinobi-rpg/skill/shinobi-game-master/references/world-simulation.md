# World Simulation

Offscreen does not mean frozen. Factions, markets, households, projects, recovery, contracts, tournaments, outlaw activity and government response advance when their registered causal boundaries become due.

Use event-seeking delivery: internal maintenance remains internal; meaningful reports/public consequences may surface softly; hard protected decisions stop automatic continuation.

Faction capability comes from conserved people, training, doctrine, equipment, infrastructure, money, location, relationships and history. Small organizations may grow; major organizations may decline. No institution has plot immunity.


Institutional work is also causal world simulation. House needs can produce player-facing assignment offers; NPC factions continue to act autonomously. Player-faction rescues and treaty defense obligations stop at the protected decision instead of silently spending Wei's House resources. Allied aid is always exact mobilization and travel, never an abstract force modifier.


## Institutional diplomacy

Binding treaty, restitution/tribute and prisoner-exchange proposals remain owned by the diplomacy/treasury/custody systems. A non-officeholding player may act as an envoy only under an active council-approved diplomatic mission whose faction, target, commander and exact terms match the proposal. The diplomacy result closes that mission into a compact AAR; it does not mint authority or bypass House hierarchy.

## Living-world delivery and local scene life

An autonomous world is not useful merely because offscreen state changes correctly; lawful developments must eventually reach Wei through their real report, rumor, witness, mission, market, faction or travel channels when they become player-relevant. Do not manufacture a new crisis just to prevent silence, and do not leave a meaningful completed development permanently trapped in backend state.

The opposite boundary matters too: **ordinary local human life does not require an offscreen world event**. When exact present people and current pressures already support a scene, the LLM may direct reversible conversation, interruption, work, humor, discomfort, disagreement and NPC-to-NPC interaction from those established facts. Python should simulate hard world change and deliver causal evidence; it should not pre-script the room merely to make it feel alive.
