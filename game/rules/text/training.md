# Training and Development

Representation is not a training multiplier. Exact people, rostered cohorts, and aggregate personnel use the same underlying development law. Compression may reduce calculation/storage cost but may not increase training speed, experience, recovery, qualification, or resource efficiency.

Trainable capability values and learning aptitudes are nonnegative and have no universal hard maximum. The comparison bands are not rank labels: roughly 100 is professional, 120 advanced, 150 elite, 170 legendary, 185 transcendent, and 200 god-tier or an exceptional world outlier. A character may be extraordinary in one capability while remaining ordinary in others.

## Ordinary training boundary

Routine practice, instruction, drills, conditioning, study, sparring, and ordinary autonomous training may raise an integer capability to 160. They may not raise it beyond 160, regardless of aptitude, hours accumulated, representation, or elapsed calendar time.

This is a progression topology rule rather than a larger point cost. Legendary capability must not emerge merely because a scheduler ran for enough years. Aggregate and roster-cohort training therefore stop at the same routine ceiling and cannot manufacture legendary means or hidden exceptional individuals.

`state/development/banks.json` stores sparse residual development credit keyed by authoritative owner and exact capability target. A bank stores earned fractional development, never unearned scheduled hours. At the routine ceiling residual preparation is bounded below one additional point; blocked decades cannot be banked into an instant legendary jump.

## Exceptional breakthroughs

Advancement beyond routine training is exact-person only and consumes persisted causal evidence through `breakthrough_resolution`. The evidence must already exist in authoritative world history and must name the exact person as an actor. Callers cannot assert a result, invent an event, or convert routine elapsed time into exceptional progress.

Each resolution advances exactly one selected capability point and records the consumed event on that person's development-bank entry. The same person cannot reuse that event for another capability. Shared missions remain shared experience: another participant may independently consume the same event for their own qualifying breakthrough.

The generic thresholds are deliberately severe:

- current capability 160-169: at least B-rank mission-grade experience, or genuinely dangerous exact combat with material injury/capture/death consequences;
- current capability 170-179: at least A-rank mission-grade experience;
- current capability 180-184: S-rank mission-grade experience;
- current capability 185 or higher: generic mission/combat evidence is insufficient. A more specific exceptional subsystem or world fact must authorize further advancement.

Operational capabilities may use qualifying mission settlement or combat experience. Martial skills, attributes, chakra dimensions, domain proficiencies, and method mastery require qualifying exact combat evidence. This prevents paperwork or routine mission completion from turning physical or technical capability into a legendary stat.

Existing exceptional values are historical truth and are not reduced by this rule. The rule governs future advancement only.

## Development settlement

On settlement, determine the owner's unresolved interval from its authoritative cursor. Split the interval at every injury, assignment, instructor, facility, equipment, doctrine, resource, mission, or other causal change that affects eligibility. Resolve only actual eligible activity, add resulting development credit to the matching target, and advance the cursor only through settled time.

A `resolved_through` cursor is proof of settlement, never a mirror of global campaign time. Global time may advance beyond a lazily represented owner, cohort, formation, or institution while a covering causal host proves that interval safe. The owner's cursor may advance only after every elapsed-time subsystem authoritative for that owner has resolved the interval or explicitly established zero eligible activity.

For an integer capability below the routine ceiling, repeatedly consume the required development credit for each whole point earned, recomputing cost after each point. Preserve the bounded nonnegative residual. Credits never move between owners or capability targets.

## Autonomous team training

A recurring exact-team review may resolve routine organized training only from saved policy and current causal state. Generic defaults are modest organized-team blocks, not total work-week limits: standard mission teams use 8 active team-training hours per weekly review, special-mission and ANBU cells use 12, temporary task forces and untyped teams use 6. Targets rotate across role-relevant capability areas rather than grinding one stat forever.

The default is only an opportunity. An active mission or team assignment preempts routine training. The team must be active. A registered non-player instructor must be available and co-located. Each trained member must be active, healthy, co-located, and have an unsettled compatible development interval. Player characters are never silently included in autonomous team training or used as autonomous instructors.

A player-led team can therefore keep developing offscreen when a lawful non-player instructor or deputy is already registered. If no such instructor is registered, the team receives no invented credit. A standing preference for future supervision is not the same as a committed instructor appointment.

Solo named-NPC training is not inferred merely from existence or spare calendar time. It requires a persisted personal, organizational, or other causal policy before an autonomous host may spend that person's offscreen time. Person continuity by itself records life/career passage and is not free stat progression.

Missions, deployments, injuries, medical restrictions, travel, commitments, institutional duties, instructor/facility availability, and other causal boundaries outrank routine training. Mission and combat experience may later support exact-person breakthrough resolution, but training never substitutes for mission experience.

Training changes capability. Promotion changes institutional status. Capability growth alone does not promote an entire formation. Rare, elite, politically important, or uniquely consequential personnel should be represented at progressively finer resolution, including exact persistent people where identity matters.

## Training facilities

When training names a mechanical facility, the runtime consumes that facility's registered training quality, capacity, and supported training categories. A facility cannot grant a training category it does not support merely because narration describes suitable space.

Facility capacity bounds the mechanically resolved session; it is not a universal reservation calendar for every background user. Ordinary ambient training locations need no mutable facility owner unless a persistent quality/capacity constraint changes an outcome.
