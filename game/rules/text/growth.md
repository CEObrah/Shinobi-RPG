# Growth and Development

Use `game/data/mechanics/training.json` and `game/data/development/model.json`. Exact people, rostered cohorts, and aggregate personnel use the same underlying development law. Representation efficiency is 1.0 unless an authoritative mechanic explicitly states otherwise; compression never grants faster growth.

Training consumes real time and any required instructor, facility, equipment, health, recovery, authority, and attendance capacity. A group training action may be settled once for the represented group, but no person receives duplicate individual and aggregate credit for the same attended interval.

Institutional promotion is separate from capability growth. A whole formation does not gain rank because time passed. Qualified people or conserved subsets change rank/status through the career and institutional authority systems.
