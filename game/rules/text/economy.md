# Economy

Ryō is conserved state. A price, salary, mission reward, project cost, purchase agreement, or narration never creates currency. Every payment names a real source account and a real destination account, and the source must hold enough currency when the transfer occurs.

`game/data/mechanics/economy.json` is the mechanical authority for public item prices, service prices, mission fee bands, salary references, living-cost references, macro finance timing, and capital-value anchors. Other files may describe wealth or prestige, but they do not override those values.

## Personal spending

Personal accounts are exact only when individual money matters to play. Ordinary shop revenue, wages, meals, rent, payroll, taxes, and household consumption for background NPCs are absorbed into aggregate background-economy budgets rather than creating personal or business ledgers.

Open-market and controlled goods may be negotiated only inside their registered price bands. Institutional-only and not-for-sale goods cannot be purchased through ordinary commerce.

Food, lodging, baths, ordinary instruction, treatment, and similar services use the registered service-price authority. A service purchase debits the exact payer, while an ordinary provider may settle into its aggregate local economy account instead of receiving a permanent personal wallet.

## Mission equipment and pay

An official village or institutional mission issues its approved standard equipment and ordinary mission consumables from conserved institutional stock. The participating shinobi does not personally buy routine issued kunai, shuriken, wire, smoke bombs, rations, standard armor, or other approved mission kit merely to perform official duty.

Issued stock is still conserved. Consumption, loss, capture, destruction, return, and refit mutate the institutional stock or holder state normally. Personally chosen upgrades, luxury gear, private travel, off-duty purchases, and personal replacement of non-issued property are personal expenses unless another lawful payer funds them.

Mission cash rewards are compensation, not reimbursement for normal issued supplies. Every mission has a rank and any promised currency reward must fit the registered rank band. Potential currency rewards are funded into mission escrow when the mission is created. Successful settlement pays applicable rewards from escrow; unused escrow returns to the funding holder. Currency costs assessed to participants transfer from the participant to the mission funding holder.

A mission cannot promise money that its funding account cannot escrow.

## Institutions, villages, countries, and houses

Country treasuries, village or military treasuries, houses, player-relevant personal accounts, and aggregate background economies are separate holders. Routine taxes, public spending, payroll, mission-market turnover, shop revenue, and ordinary household consumption are netted into background-budget transfers. Strategically meaningful transfers such as village subsidies, named institutional contracts, mission escrow, major projects, and exact player salary remain explicit.

Macro finance settles at the registered monthly boundary. A short payer pays only what it actually holds. Unpaid obligations become arrears instead of creating negative money. Later periods may repay arrears only within the registered catch-up limit after the current obligation is considered.

Institutional projects consume their registered material stock and pay their registered contractor cost before work begins. Authorizing a project does not create a completed facility or free resources.

Capital values are planning and replacement-value anchors. They are not automatic sale prices and do not imply a liquid buyer exists.

## Sparse accounting

The economy is exact where a gameplay decision depends on the exact holder, asset, reward, purchase, treasury, project, or estate. Diffuse background commerce remains aggregate. A restaurant does not need a full business ledger merely because a meal has a price, and a village does not need one personal wallet for every resident merely because the village economy is simulated.
