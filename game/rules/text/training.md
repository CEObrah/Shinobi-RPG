# Training and Development

Representation is not a training multiplier. Exact people, rostered cohorts, and aggregate personnel use the same underlying development law. Compression may reduce calculation/storage cost but may not increase training speed, experience, recovery, qualification, or resource efficiency.

Trainable capability values and learning aptitudes are nonnegative and have no hard maximum. `200` is only an ordinary-reference/outlier threshold. Earned progression above 200 continues under the same diminishing-returns and point-cost laws unless a more specific subsystem explicitly defines a separate bounded quantity.

`state/development/banks.json` stores sparse residual development credit keyed by authoritative owner and exact capability target. A bank stores earned fractional development, never unearned scheduled hours.

On settlement, determine the owner's unresolved interval from its authoritative cursor. Split the interval at every injury, assignment, instructor, facility, equipment, doctrine, resource, mission, or other causal change that affects eligibility. Resolve only actual eligible activity, add resulting development credit to the matching target, and advance the cursor only through settled time.

For an integer capability, repeatedly consume the required development credit for each whole point earned, recomputing cost after each point. Preserve the nonnegative residual. Credits never move between owners or capability targets.

For aggregate personnel, the same law applies. Residual credits remain tied to the relevant aggregate owner/target. Capability changes update the authoritative aggregate capability source and invalidate any derived combat kernel. Qualified-subset promotion is a conserved personnel transfer, not a fractional bank effect. Routine training does not manufacture field, combat, or command experience.

Roster cohorts use the same law while preserving stable represented identities. A shared training block may update a cohort once rather than fanning out identical writes to every person. Any individually divergent component settles only its actual attended hours and may not receive both cohort credit and duplicate personal credit.

Offscreen settlement may be lazy, but eligible elapsed work may not disappear. Before a capability-dependent resolution, settle the relevant owner's unresolved development interval through the normal causal scheduler.

Training changes capability. Promotion changes institutional status. Capability growth alone does not promote an entire formation. Rare, elite, politically important, or uniquely consequential personnel should be represented at progressively finer resolution, including exact persistent people where identity matters.

## Training facilities

When training names a mechanical facility, the runtime consumes that facility's registered training quality, capacity, and supported training categories. A facility cannot grant a training category it does not support merely because narration describes suitable space.

Facility capacity bounds the mechanically resolved session; it is not a universal reservation calendar for every background user. Ordinary ambient training locations need no mutable facility owner unless a persistent quality/capacity constraint changes an outcome.
