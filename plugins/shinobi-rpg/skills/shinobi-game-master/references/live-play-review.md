# Live-Play Review

Treat real play as integration testing. Flag false truth, broken agency/knowledge, conservation violations, exploits, dead scheduler delivery, physically impossible combat, repetitive loops, shallow choices, or needless context cost. Valid-but-underdeveloped mechanics may also be a finding when they materially reduce tactical choice, causal flow, world vitality, clarity, or long-campaign reliability.

After every live gameplay turn, append exactly one concise `OOC QA:` line after the IC result. If play exposed a material reusable issue/opportunity, report only the strongest one: observed symptom, player impact, likely authoritative owner, and smallest coherent fix or focused regression. If nothing material was observed, say `OOC QA: No material improvement identified this turn.` Never manufacture a problem, repeat an unchanged issue as though new, or turn play into a development changelog.

Prefer the smallest coherent source fix. Do not manufacture story to hide mechanical starvation. The QA line is diagnosis only. Ordinary play never edits source or campaign truth; implementation requires explicit `OOC DEV:` intent.
