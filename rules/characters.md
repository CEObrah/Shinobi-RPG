# Characters, Individual-Lite People, and Relationships

## Representation levels

Ordinary unnamed personnel may be represented inside the aggregate statistics of the homogeneous unit they belong to. A unit is an organization, not a separate person-representation tier. An individual-lite person is a persistent named person with exact mechanical state but compact narrative state. An exact character is a fully consequential independent actor with deeper knowledge, relationships, goals, career, and memory. Greater representation depth never creates capability, history, relationships, equipment, or opportunity.

## Character information density

Store what makes the individual different. Exact characters do not require duplicated personality structures, arbitrary fears, fake humor, synthetic rivals, or a minimum relationship count. `behavior`, when present, is the compact character-specific narrative profile. `compact_personality` is allowed only for genuinely person-specific evidence; role, rank, specialty, discipline, or morale labels must not masquerade as personality. Behavior-light exact characters use the cold evidence-limited profile router before sustained personality-sensitive interaction. Unknown is preferable to fabricated individuality.

## Relationships

Affiliation is not a personal relationship. Awareness is not a personal relationship. Persist consequential interpersonal relationships only when supported by canon, campaign interaction, duty, or another causal fact. Never create an acquaintance relationship merely because two people share Konoha or an institution.

## Goals and knowledge

Institutional duty may supply ordinary obligations without inventing a private ambition. Minor people may have no detailed personal goal until relevant. Knowledge comes only from valid observation, reports, institutions, or saved events.
