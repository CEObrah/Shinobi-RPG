## Combat objective

Render combat as fast, dangerous problem-solving between trained people. Preserve runtime results exactly while making the battlefield readable enough that the player can make intelligent decisions.

Never turn combat into alternating attack summaries, hidden-stat exposition, or decorative anime spectacle. Every important action should have a physical cause, path, counter, effect, and changed situation.

## Spatial ledger

Maintain an implicit spatial ledger throughout the scene. Keep the following legible whenever causal:

- who can perceive whom;
- meaningful distance and closing speed;
- elevation;
- line of sight;
- cover and concealment;
- walls, doors, trees, roofs, corridors, water, slopes, and other terrain;
- exits and escape routes;
- civilians, captives, objectives, or fragile infrastructure;
- allies and opponents;
- weapons and dropped tools;
- hazards created by previous techniques;
- the position change created by the last exchange.

Do not repeat the whole ledger every turn. Mention only what matters to the current exchange and decision.

If exact geometry is unavailable, preserve qualitative spatial truth instead of inventing measurements.

## Causal exchange order

When the runtime result makes the sequence relevant, narrate roughly in this order:

1. observable initiation or recognition;
2. movement and commitment;
3. technique, weapon, grapple, or maneuver startup;
4. path, timing, and obstruction;
5. counter, interruption, evasion, block, or failed response;
6. contact or miss;
7. injury, cost, equipment, terrain, or objective consequence;
8. new positions and tactical state;
9. genuine player decision.

Do not force every exchange into nine explicit paragraphs. Preserve the causal order in natural prose.

A declared target is never an automatic hit. A successful defense is not automatically effortless. A miss still has a physical explanation consistent with the resolved result.

## Intelligent opponents

Let opponents use only knowledge, capability, doctrine, equipment, relationships, authority, and objectives they actually possess.

Show competence through:

- foot placement;
- delayed commitment;
- angle control;
- feints supported by actual action;
- tool preparation;
- economical seals;
- use of cover;
- attention to teammates;
- exploitation of an established injury;
- retreat or disengagement when rational;
- refusal to expose a route or objective;
- attempts to protect, capture, delay, escape, observe, or destroy according to actual goals.

Do not make enemies stupid so Wei can shine. Do not make them omniscient so the fight stays difficult. They cannot react to information they do not have.

Do not narrate hidden intent as fact. Render observable behavior and Wei-supported inference: "He keeps yielding toward the east stair" is valid; "He intends to lure you into the archive" is not unless Wei knows or has enough evidence to infer it.

## Techniques and tools

Treat every technique as a physical/tactical system when the mechanic makes these relevant:

- preparation or seals;
- startup;
- range and path;
- line of sight;
- area;
- speed and timing;
- cost;
- counter conditions;
- collision or interference;
- collateral;
- evidence;
- aftermath.

Name techniques only when Wei recognizes them or learns the name through a valid channel. Otherwise describe observable effects.

Use shinobi tools as real objects. Kunai, wire, tags, smoke, medicine, blades, armor, containers, puppets, summons, and equipment occupy space, can be lost or damaged when the runtime says so, and may create evidence or constraints.

Avoid generic luminous-power language when the effect is materially specific.

## Injury, fatigue, and resources

Give injuries functional consequences only when runtime state supports them. A damaged leg can alter stance or movement. A hand injury can affect grip or seals. Blood loss, pain, impaired vision, damaged armor, or breathing problems matter when mechanically established.

Do not add injuries for flavor. Do not erase them because the next scene would be easier without them.

Render chakra and fatigue through lived scale when exact numbers are not necessary. Use exact values only when the player asks OOC or the value is directly decision-relevant and player-visible.

Register persistent aftermath after violence when established:

- wounds;
- deaths;
- missing tools;
- damaged terrain or property;
- witnesses;
- evidence;
- noise or alarms;
- custody;
- mission-objective changes;
- reports and obligations.

## Teams and multiple actors

Keep role and coordination legible without narrating every participant equally.

Emphasize:

- who creates openings;
- who protects whom;
- overlapping or conflicting lanes;
- signals and doctrine;
- broken formation or separation;
- rescue and extraction routes;
- friendly-fire or civilian constraints when established;
- command and delegated initiative.

Do not make teammates freeze while Wei acts unless the runtime says they cannot act. Do not make them execute unpersisted telepathic coordination.

When team familiarity is low, show the resolved coordination problem through timing, spacing, signal recognition, redundant effort, or missed exploitation rather than a floating stat label.

## Ambush, stealth, and uncertainty

Preserve perception and information boundaries before initiative-like narration.

Do not announce an attacker, trap, clone, transformation, concealed weapon, poison, or technique before Wei lawfully detects its relevant signs.

When danger is only partially perceived, describe what Wei actually receives: sound, movement, disturbed dust, missing patrol noise, a wire glint, pressure change, a teammate's signal, or an impact without source.

If Wei is surprised, narrate the consequences of that surprise without retroactively granting him warning.

If Wei successfully surprises someone, do not give the target knowledge merely to make the exchange more cinematic.

## Large-battle zoom

Narrate aggregate combat at the scale the runtime resolved.

For formations and large forces, emphasize:

- sectors and fronts;
- terrain and routes;
- command intent;
- reserves;
- cohesion and morale;
- supply or support when mechanically relevant;
- casualties and retreat;
- objectives such as hold, break through, delay, encircle, extract, or withdraw.

Do not fabricate thousands of individual duels.

Wake named exact actors narratively only when the runtime zoom boundary makes their individual actions causal. Reconcile their consequences into the parent battle exactly once.

## Decision boundary

Once an exchange creates a genuine unresolved player decision, stop before choosing Wei's follow-through.

Use `references/choices.md` for choices. In active combat, the default horizon becomes:

- Choices 1 to 3: immediate responses for the next exchange;
- Choices 4 and 5: wider tactical objectives for the next several exchanges or battle objective;
- Choice 6: Free Action.

Do not offer long-term administration during immediate danger. Do not offer a menu if the player already declared Wei's action.


## Persistent operational battlefields

When a large conflict contains simultaneous sectors, divisions, reserves, or reinforcements, use the runtime's persistent battlefield layer above exact combat. The battlefield belongs to an existing conflict/front and owns sector geometry, formation assignment, orders, timed redeployment, pressure, and communication delay. It does **not** own exact wounds, deaths, casualties, technique outcomes, territory, or force creation.

A formation redeploying between sectors is physically in transit and cannot contribute at either endpoint until arrival. Other sectors continue to progress while Wei travels, fights, waits, or races ahead. Named shinobi and exact teams may zoom into exact combat while their parent formation remains conserved; exact consequences reconcile back into the aggregate battle once and never duplicate bodies, wounds, resource costs, or contribution.

Battlefield knowledge obeys perception and communications. Direct sight, sensors, scouts, messenger shinobi, summons, or saved communication channels may deliver different information at different delays. Never expose raw offscreen enemy pressure merely because the runtime stores it. A delivered report may interrupt time advancement when it creates a real player decision; unseen sectors must not freeze just because Wei is elsewhere.

## Weather, sensing, and elemental affordances

Derived environment modifies physical channels only through registered mechanics. Visibility can affect visual detection; rain/noise can mask ordinary sound; ground/weather can alter track evidence and scent reliability. Chakra sensing and barrier channels remain weather-neutral by default unless a separate exact mechanic explicitly says otherwise.

Surface water, conductivity, fire spread and smoke/gas drift are environmental affordances, not automatic jutsu outcomes. A technique/hazard owner must explicitly consume the relevant channel before the result changes. Water techniques whose registered medium explicitly permits external water may reduce only their material-generation burden when lawful surface water is available; this is never a generic damage bonus or free chakra. Conductivity and smoke/gas drift remain inert until a specific registered mechanic consumes them.

For security networks, use only detection channels that the persisted sector actually materialized. Weather modifies the best available independent channel rather than averaging channels in a way that makes added sensors harmful. Fog can hinder eyes without making a chakra barrier blind. Current sector establishment pays for barrier/chakra coverage; physical patrol, scent, auditory, or tracking channels require their own resource-backed representation before they may be claimed.

## Large-war continuity

At formation scale, preserve persistent operational memory and communication/battlefield structure while exact combat remains the authority for casualties and named-person consequences. Use battlefield sectors, sensor/messenger latency, reserves and redeployment to structure large engagements; zoom to exact teams/people only where individual causality can change the result, then reconcile back into the same conserved formation/population truth.
