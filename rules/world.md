# Routes, Travel, Missions, Diplomacy and War

Route state is authoritative in `state/world/routes-and-settlements.json`. Deterministic route travel uses `data/mechanics/travel.json`. Military action additionally requires real personnel, orders, supplies, command, communication and political authority.

Missions require an actual causal owner, client/authority, scope, resources and terms. Mobilization and reinforcement consume real travel and communication time. Occupation, siege, contracts and route disruption persist in state. Ordinary settlements may materialize only from existing country population owners; hidden villages cannot be invented.
